About Us

A family-owned repair business built on honest work, fair pricing and pride in doing the job right.

Meet Jason

Experience You Can Trust

Jason has more than 14 years of experience working on heavy-duty equipment, diesel trucks, automotive vehicles, RVs and trailers. His hands-on knowledge allows him to diagnose problems carefully and find dependable solutions.

Penny's Heavy Duty & Auto was started with a simple goal: provide honest repairs, quality workmanship and fair pricing for every customer.

Based in Prince George, BC, Jason brings the same care and attention to every job, whether it is a routine repair or a major mechanical project.

Our Story

Honest Repairs Without the Runaround

We understand how stressful vehicle and equipment problems can be. Customers deserve clear answers, honest recommendations and repairs they can depend on.

At Penny's, the goal is never to sell unnecessary work. Jason takes the time to investigate the problem, explain what he finds and help customers understand their repair options.

Whether the job involves a family vehicle, work truck, diesel equipment or travel trailer, it receives the same careful workmanship and attention to detail.

Family Owned

Built on Honest Work

Penny's Heavy Duty & Auto is more than a repair business. It is something our family is building together.

Around the shop, you may find one of the kids handing over tools, helping clean up or learning how things work. We believe in teaching the value of hard work, honesty and taking pride in doing a job properly.

Those same values guide how we treat every customer and every vehicle that comes through the shop.

Teaching the next generation the value of honest work—one job at a time.
